# 迷途之子 — interface direction

The conversation is a visual-novel scene about finding company while unsure of the way forward. It draws on MyGO's emotional premise without copying official layouts, logos, or backgrounds.

## Visual system

- Blue-hour city after rain: deep navy UI, storm-blue panels, pale-cyan interactive highlights, and a small warm light in the illustrated scene.
- The original overpass illustration is scenery only. Live2D characters render separately and remain the focus.
- Interface typography is a clear sans-serif. A single substantial dialogue line has priority over labels or decorative copy.
- Compact rounded rectangles and fine cool borders replace the former floral cards, motifs, and cream surfaces.

## Screen composition

- Left rail: project title, five selectable companions, and a short grounding line. At most two characters share the scene.
- Main stage: rain-washed city background, two Live2D performers, outfit controls above, and a dark translucent dialogue box over the lower edge.
- A speaker tab identifies each turn. The small downward triangle appears only when another line is pending; the whole dialogue box also advances that line.
- Input stays directly below the stage. Character motion and existing prototype conversation behavior remain unchanged by this theme.

## Boundaries

The stage background and route-arrow mark are original project assets. Character models are local demonstration assets; do not bundle or redistribute them until usage rights are confirmed.
